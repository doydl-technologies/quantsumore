# Contributing

`quantsumore` is retired and no longer accepts issues, pull requests, feature
requests, or other contributions. Submitted work may not be reviewed or merged.

The source remains available under the Apache License 2.0. Anyone who wants to
continue development may create an independent fork, subject to the license and
the terms of the third-party services used by the software.

See [ARCHIVED.md](ARCHIVED.md) for the complete retirement notice.
